Frequently asked questions

What clients ask before we start.

Which technologies do you use?
On the application side: React, Next.js, Angular, Node.js and Java / Spring Boot, with REST or GraphQL APIs and PostgreSQL. On the operations side: Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, GitLab CI or GitHub Actions pipelines, and deployment on AWS or Azure.
Do you work in Paris or remotely?
I am based in the Paris region and work in Paris and its surroundings, on site or remotely. For projects outside the region, the work is done remotely with regular check-ins.
Can you take a project from end to end?
Yes, that is the core of my practice: design, API, interface, database, CI/CD pipeline, cloud deployment and monitoring. You have a single point of contact from scoping through to production.
Can you take over an existing application?
Yes. I have delivered handovers of existing codebases, data migrations onto new schemas and CI/CD setups on applications already in service, with cut-over and no downtime.
What is your experience?
Six years as an employed developer at Olinn, G-ON and then Amadeus, on business applications, high-volume platforms and cloud delivery pipelines, before founding Consaltia.
