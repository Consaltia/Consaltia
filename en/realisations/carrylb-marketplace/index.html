Marketplace

CarryLB, a marketplace for travellers and senders

A marketplace does not sell a service, it sells trust between two strangers who are going to arrange to meet.

Context

CarryLB connects travellers who have spare room in their luggage with people who want a parcel carried between two cities or countries. The service is live at carrylb.com. Like any marketplace, it has to solve a cold-start problem: without travellers no sender comes, and the reverse.

The problem

Beyond the cold start, the central difficulty is trust.

  • Two strangers have to agree to meet and hand over a parcel
  • The sender does not want to pay before handover, the traveller does not want to carry without a payment guarantee
  • Search has to cross a route, dates and available capacity, which is harder than keyword search
  • Disputes, rare but inevitable, have to be arbitrable on evidence

The solution

A platform built around the listing and booking pair, with payment held until the parcel is handed over.

  • Trip listings with origin, destination, dates and available capacity
  • Search crossing route, period and parcel weight
  • Profiles with history and reciprocal reviews after each transport
  • Built-in messaging, so exchanges stay recorded on the platform
  • Payment held and released on confirmation of handover
  • Booking summary detailing price, weight and itinerary

My role

Product and data model design, API, interface, search engine, messaging, escrowed payment handling and cloud deployment.

Constraints

  • Payment has to be held then released, which requires a more complex flow than a direct payment
  • Search has to stay relevant with few listings, the situation of the first months
  • Exchanges have to stay on the platform to be usable in a dispute
  • Two sides to grow, with different interfaces and expectations
  • International context: currencies, time zones, address formats

Outcome

A live marketplace where the full journey, publish a trip, be found, book, talk and get paid, works end to end.

  • Escrowed payment removes the main objection on both sides
  • Reciprocal reviews build the reputation that makes the service usable
  • Recorded messaging gives something to arbitrate a dispute with

Outcomes are described by the capability delivered. No commercial performance metric is claimed here: usage figures belong to the client, and I do not publish numbers I cannot substantiate.
