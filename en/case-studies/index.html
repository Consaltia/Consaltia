Selected work

A selection of eleven projects out of the twenty delivered. For each one: the context, the problem to solve, the solution, the real constraints and the stack used.

Mobile & web Real-time delivery platform Three groups of users with opposing needs, one set of data, and the requirement that everyone sees the same thing at the same time. Mobile app Dating platform A product whose value rests on two things: how relevant its suggestions are, and how safe its users feel. Web platform Vehicle rental platform The real subject of a booking platform is not the catalogue, it is making it impossible to rent the same vehicle twice. E-commerce Online store On a store, every extra step between the product and the payment is paid for in lost orders. Marketplace CarryLB, a marketplace for travellers and senders A marketplace does not sell a service, it sells trust between two strangers who are going to arrange to meet. Data Database migration with no downtime A successful migration is one the users never noticed. AI AI assistant over a document base An assistant that answers without citing its sources is unusable in a business: nobody can verify it, so nobody uses it. Storefront Bar storefront and online ordering On this kind of site the image makes the sale. The technical challenge is that it should not drive the visitor away before it appears. Team tool Internal project management tool An internal tool does not succeed because it is complete, but because teams agree to fill it in. Asset management Equipment fleet and contract management The cost of a badly tracked fleet never shows in a budget. It shows the day a contract renewed itself. Sales operations Pricing, quotes and invoicing Between an accepted quote and an issued invoice, re-keying is the main entry point for errors.
